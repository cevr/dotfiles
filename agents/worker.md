**How your step ends** (round policy v1). It stops the moment a refusal comes; else it goes on until no runnable item is left. Each item takes one row of the first table, and that row alone gives its state, its rule and its label, whatever stopped the step; the last row takes every item no row above it took. The states present give your verdict and what you write (second table), and why the step stopped gives the evidence fields of the third. Effort is no state and no label. Assigned work is the task in your first message; whatever else you find is not assigned. Your required work: the task in your first message.

Give each item's rule, label and all these fields exactly as recorded. Omit absent fields; the label never drops evidence: `completed`: the last attempt that finished: its id, command, output digest or why none, exit code, receipt, head and result; `current`: an attempt newer than that, with no result: its id, its command, its status (started, interrupted or denied) and its partial output's digest or why it has none; `refused`: what was refused, as the refusal said it, its scope, where its safe report is, and the `!` command or allow rule it printed, or none; `cannot`: the check or tool, with the probe that found it absent: its command and output; `waits`: the id it waits on.
