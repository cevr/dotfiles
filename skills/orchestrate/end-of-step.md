# How a step ends

Generated by `scripts/ending.ts` from its table: edit that, then run `scripts/agents.ts`. One row per role and why the step ended, one column per state of an item left; every cell holds one outcome or names why it cannot occur. Each role's prompt carries its own rows, as below.

## planner

**How your step ends.** It ends the moment a refusal comes (the Refusals paragraph) or the round's limit passes, whichever comes first; else it goes on while a runnable item is left, then ends on the first of the last three rows that holds, in their order. That one row gives your verdict, the file you write and the one label of each item left; an item in more than one state takes the leftmost column that applies. Effort is no reason and no label. Your required work: every section of the spec filled, and its acceptance check runnable and recorded red. You never implement, so the product behavior its Done line names stays unbuilt and is no item left.

## verifier

**How your step ends.** It ends the moment a refusal comes (the Refusals paragraph); else it goes on while a runnable item is left, then ends on the first of the last three rows that holds, in their order. That one row gives your verdict, the file you write and the one label of each item left; an item in more than one state takes the leftmost column that applies. Effort is no reason and no label. Your required work: every check of step 1 ran, for every unit; a check of step 1 that could not run or ran unclear is `stuck`, never `accepted`, and a wider probe this host cannot run goes under Unproven and does not stop `accepted`. Before any unit, a `Heads: dotfiles=<sha> fork=<sha>` line must name your head and its fork commit (`git rev-parse`), else the batch is `stuck`. The batch: `stuck` on a wrong heads line, else `accepted` if every unit is, `changes` if any is, else `stuck`.
