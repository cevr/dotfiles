## Compass Direction Symbolism

```
           NORTH
          (Eagle/God)
            Dan
             │
  WEST ─────┼───── EAST
  (Bull/     │      (Lion/
  Sacrifice) │      King)
  Ephraim    │      Judah
             │
           SOUTH
          (Man/Man)
          Reuben
```

| Direction | Creature | Aspect | Significance |
|---|---|---|---|
| North | Eagle | God/Divinity | God's throne (Isa 14:13; Ps 48:2) |
| South | Man | Humanity | Man-centered; rebellion from south (Korah — Num 16) |
| East | Lion | Kingship | Sun rises; Christ comes from east (Matt 24:27) |
| West | Bull | Sacrifice | Toward the temple entrance (tabernacle faced east) |

### Directional Movement in Prophecy
- **Ram pushes west, north, south** (Dan 8:4) — from east (Persia). Does NOT push east (toward God's throne direction varies by framework)
- **Little horn pushes south, east, pleasant land** (Dan 8:9) — from north (claiming God's position)
- **Korah's rebellion from south of camp** (Num 16) — rebellion from the "man" direction

| Sabbath (7th day) | Sunday worship (1st day) |
| 777 (Sabbath character) | 666 (mark of the beast) |
| Lamb (Christ) | Lamb-like beast (Rev 13:11) |
| True prophet | False prophet |
| Jerusalem (city of God) | Babylon (city of confusion) |
| Seal of God (forehead) | Mark of the beast (forehead/hand) |
| 144,000 (sealed) | Those who receive the mark |
| Christ as King-Priest | Papal claim to kingship + priesthood |
| Marriage (Gen 2) | Counterfeits of marriage |
| Tree of Life | Tree of knowledge (counterfeit wisdom) |
| 4 living creatures (God's throne) | 4 beasts (Daniel 7 — Satan's kingdoms) |
| Ram (Christ-type sacrifice) | He-goat (Satan-type — Lev 17:7 goats = devils) |
