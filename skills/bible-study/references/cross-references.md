## OT-NT Typological Fulfillment

Old Testament "types" are fulfilled by New Testament "antitypes."

### Major Type-Antitype Pairs
| OT Type | NT Antitype | Key Texts |
|---|---|---|
| Passover Lamb | Christ crucified | Exodus 12; 1 Cor 5:7; John 1:29 |
| Day of Atonement | Investigative Judgment | Leviticus 16; Daniel 8:14; Hebrews 9 |
| High Priest | Christ as High Priest | Leviticus 16; Hebrews 4:14-16; 8:1 |
| Cyrus destroys Babylon | Christ destroys spiritual Babylon | Isaiah 45:1; Rev 16:12; 18:2 |
| Cyrus restores Jerusalem | Christ brings New Jerusalem | Ezra 1:1-3; Rev 21:2 |
| Eve from Adam's side | Church from Christ's pierced side | Gen 2:21-22; John 19:34 |
| Ram (Day of Atonement) | Christ as sacrifice | Lev 16:3,5; Dan 8:3-4; Isa 45:1 |
| Scapegoat (Azazel) | Satan bearing guilt | Lev 16:8-10,20-22 |
| Manna | Christ, Bread of Life | Exodus 16; John 6:31-35 |
| Bronze serpent | Christ lifted up | Numbers 21:9; John 3:14 |
| Joseph (dreamer, to Egypt) | Jesus (dreams, to Egypt) | Genesis 37-50; Matthew 1-2 |

### Method
1. Identify an OT event, person, or ritual
2. Look for NT passages that explicitly quote or reference it
3. Note what is the SAME (confirming the connection) and what is DIFFERENT (showing the upgrade)
4. The difference between type and antitype is always an escalation — the antitype is greater

## Prophetic Recapitulation

The same historical sequence told multiple times through different symbols.

### Daniel's Four Recapitulations
| Daniel 2 | Daniel 7 | Daniel 8 | Daniel 11 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Gold | Lion | — | — |
| Silver | Bear | Ram (two horns) | Kings of Persia |
| Bronze | Leopard | He-goat (notable horn) | King of Grecia |
| Iron | 4th beast (iron teeth) | Little horn | King of North |
| Iron + Clay | 10 horns + little horn | — | — |
| Stone/Mountain | Ancient of Days / Son of Man | 2300 days cleansing | Michael stands up |

Each retelling adds detail. Daniel 2 = overview. Daniel 7 = adds judgment. Daniel 8 = adds sanctuary.
Daniel 11 = adds historical detail.

### Revelation's Recapitulations
| Churches (2-3) | Seals (6-8) | Trumpets (8-11) | Beasts (12-14) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ephesus | White horse | — | — |
| Smyrna | Red horse | — | — |
| Pergamos | Black horse | — | — |
| Thyatira | Pale horse | — | Dragon/Beast |
| Sardis | Souls under altar | — | — |
| Philadelphia | Signs in heaven | — | — |
| Laodicea | Silence | 7th trumpet | Three Angels |

### Method
1. When encountering a prophetic sequence, check if the same sequence appears elsewhere
2. Align parallel symbols using shared characteristics
3. New details in each retelling expand the picture — they don't contradict

## Prophetic Timeline

### Day-Year Principle
1 prophetic day = 1 literal year

**Biblical basis:**
- Numbers 14:34 — "each day for a year... forty days, forty years"
- Ezekiel 4:6 — "I have appointed thee each day for a year"

### Key Time Prophecies
| Prophecy | Calculation | Start | End |
|---|---|---|---|
| 70 weeks (Daniel 9:24) | 70 x 7 = 490 years | 457 BC (decree of Artaxerxes) | AD 34 (stoning of Stephen) |
| 69 weeks to Messiah (Dan 9:25) | 69 x 7 = 483 years | 457 BC | AD 27 (baptism of Jesus) |
| "Midst of week" (Dan 9:27) | 3.5 years into 70th week | AD 27 | AD 31 (crucifixion) |
| 2300 days (Dan 8:14) | 2300 years | 457 BC | AD 1844 |

### The 70 Weeks "Cut Off"
The 70 weeks are "cut off" (חָתַךְ *chathak* #2852 — only use in OT) from the 2300 days.
Same starting point (457 BC), same decree. The 490 years are the first portion of the 2300.

## The Two Cities Framework

All prophecy is about two cities: **Jerusalem** and **Babylon**.

```
JERUSALEM                           BABYLON
God's city                          Satan's counterfeit
Truth                               Confusion (Babel = confound)
Freedom                             Captivity
Cyrus destroys Babylon              Christ destroys spiritual Babylon
  → restores Jerusalem                → brings New Jerusalem
```

### Application
- Judah disobeyed → sent to literal Babylon → Cyrus delivered
- Church disobeyed → spiritual Babylon → Christ delivers
- Every prophetic narrative can be located in this framework

## The Four Living Creatures Framework

Four = universality. The four living creatures of Revelation 4:7 / Ezekiel 1 map to multiple biblical groups.

| Creature | Side of Christ | Gospel | Daniel's Friends | Camp (Numbers 2) | Direction |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Lion | King | Matthew | Daniel | Judah | East |
| Calf/Bull | Sacrifice/Servant | Mark | Hananiah | Ephraim | West |
| Man | Man | Luke | Mishael | Reuben | South |
| Eagle | God | John | Azariah | Dan | North |

Three earthly creatures (lion, calf, man) = three synoptic Gospels.
One heavenly creature (eagle) = John's Gospel.

### The Anti-Four
The same pattern appears in counterfeit:
| Daniel 2 | Daniel 7 | Daniel 8 | Revelation 13 & 17 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Gold | Lion | — | Lion-mouthed beast |
| Silver | Bear | Ram | Lamb-like-horned beast |
| Bronze | Leopard | He-goat | Image beast |
| Iron | 4th beast | Little horn | Wilderness beast |

## King = Kingdom Principle

In Hebrew prophetic thought, king and kingdom are interchangeable.

- **Daniel 2:37-38** — "Thou, O king... **Thou art** this head of gold"
- **Daniel 2:39** — "after thee shall arise **another kingdom**"
- **Daniel 7:17** — "These great beasts, which are four, are four **kings**"
- **Daniel 7:23** — "The fourth beast shall be the fourth **kingdom**"

The greatest king represents the kingdom. Therefore:
- Fall of Nebuchadnezzar (Daniel 4) = Fall of Babylon
- Beast = King = Kingdom (all interchangeable)
- A tree representing a king (Daniel 4; Ezekiel 31) also represents the kingdom
